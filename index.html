Focus areas

What I work on.

Three threads, one underlying question: how do we make autonomous systems safe to rely on?

01

AI supply chain security

Where models, data and dependencies come from, what an attacker can do to that chain, and how a system built on it stays trustworthy.

02

IoT & firmware security

Firmware update mechanisms, the widening attack surface of CCTV and IoT deployments, and systematic classification of what goes wrong.

03

Safe autonomous systems

Deep learning at the boundary with cybersecurity — and the policy, governance and human questions that technical fixes alone don’t answer.
