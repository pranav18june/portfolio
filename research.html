01 Indo–Swiss grant · 2026–

AI supply chain security

AI for Public Good: Indo–Swiss Collaboration for Sustainable & Resilient Supply Chains.

An international collaboration on applying AI to supply chains that have to stay resilient under stress — and on securing the AI supply chain itself: where models, data and dependencies come from, and what an adversary can do to any link in that chain before it reaches production.

02 C3iHub, IIT Kanpur · 2025–26

Firmware update mechanisms in IoT devices

Nine months on a DST-funded IoT security initiative, hosted at IIIT Allahabad.

Analysing how deployed IoT devices receive and verify firmware updates — the step where a compromise propagates to every device in a fleet at once. CCTV and IoT ecosystems keep expanding, so systematic vulnerability classification stops being optional and starts being the only way to keep up.

  • Role Undergraduate researcher (cybersecurity), internship
  • Funding DST, Government of India & IIT Kanpur
  • Lab IoT Security Lab, IIIT Allahabad — under Prof. Venkatesan Subramanian

A wider view of security

Not onlytechnical vulnerabilities
Alsogeopolitics, national strategy, policy, governance
Andwhat individual data is worth beyond privacy

A long conversation with Dr. Gulshan Rai — India’s first National Cyber Security Coordinator and former Director General of CERT-In — during his visit to our IoT Security Lab reframed a lot of this for me. Security today tucks into economics, surveillance and global influence in ways that are easy to overlook when you are staring at a binary. I try to keep both views in scope: the vulnerability in front of me, and the system it sits inside.
