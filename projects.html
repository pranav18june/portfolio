01 Startup · wisor.in

Wisor

Hostel essentials, delivered — ₹1.5 lakh+ in sales across four days of operations.

Wisor delivers the move-in kit to students arriving at their hostel: bedding, bucket, lock, mattress — the twenty-odd things everyone needs on day one and nobody wants to go hunting for in an unfamiliar city.

The interesting constraint is that the entire market shows up at once and then disappears. There is no soft launch, no iterating your way to product-market fit, and no second attempt: stock, pricing and the delivery run all have to be right before the first student walks through the gate. We crossed ₹1.5 lakh in that window. Four days is not long enough to get lucky twice, which makes it the most honest feedback loop I have been inside.

  • Where IIIT Allahabad, Prayagraj
  • Result ₹1.5 lakh+ in sales, four days of operations
  • Live at wisor.in

02 Founder · Dec 2025 – Mar 2026

CILADO

An anonymous gifting platform, piloted on campus.

A startup idea I ran as a pilot at IIIT Allahabad across the Christmas and Valentine’s season: let people send a gift to someone on campus without revealing who it came from. Four months from idea to a live service with real orders, real logistics and real edge cases — which is where most of the actual learning was.

  • Where IIIT Allahabad, Prayagraj
  • Run as A seasonal pilot, deliberately time-boxed
